Before we begin

Good questions, clear answers.

A little context on the services, the EmpowerMed program, and what to expect from an enquiry.

Who does MedBrand work with?

We work with healthcare and wellness providers, teams, and brands looking for help with positioning, digital experiences, content, or thoughtful growth systems. We also run EmpowerMed for candidates entering remote clinic roles. Share your context and we can confirm whether there is a fit.

What is EmpowerMed?

EmpowerMed is MedBrand’s 45-day accelerator for Malagasy talent. It covers AI-driven clinic workflows, consent-first outreach, professional English, and placement preparation, with 1:1 mentorship and live roleplay. Join the WhatsApp community to get the curriculum and the orientation invite.

Do I need healthcare experience or fluent English?

No. Training starts from zero, and the first weeks are built to raise professional English from intermediate to working level. We assess your level at orientation and group training accordingly. You do need discipline and a laptop or smartphone.

Are the remote roles guaranteed?

No. The program prepares you and makes introductions to independent global clinics, but hiring decisions and final pay belong to the clinic. We do not promise a role, a salary, or a timeline.

Does MedBrand provide clinical care or medical advice?

No. MedBrand provides strategy, product, communications, and creative services, and runs the EmpowerMed training program. We do not provide diagnosis, treatment, or clinical advice, and the qualifier portal is not a clinical service.

How does the enquiry form work?

The form does not send data to this website. When you submit it, your own email app opens with a prefilled message addressed to MedBrand, and you choose whether to send it. Nothing is stored on this site. If your email app does not open, join the WhatsApp community or use the direct email link instead.

Can you guarantee a number of leads or business results?

No. Outcomes depend on the audience, offer, market, implementation, and many factors outside a single campaign. We focus on clear strategy, considered execution, and learning from agreed measures.
